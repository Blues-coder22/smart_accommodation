# Limitations (updated to match version 9.26)

Several functions fell outside the project scope because of time and finance constraints. Payments are recorded and verified by people, not processed by the system: there is no live payment gateway such as M-Pesa Daraja, and the platform never holds guests' money. Each guest pays the host's share to the host and the platform's share (service fee plus commission) to the administrator, and each owner confirms what they received, so commission does not depend on hosts remitting it. When a booking is cancelled after the platform's share was accepted, the system works out the refund due and notifies everyone, but the administrator still sends the money by hand and records it; no refunds are automatic. The commission rate, service fee, payment instructions and the booking time limits (time to enter payment details, host reply time and the free-cancellation window) are set by the administrator in the interface, and apply to new bookings.

Prices are stored and paid in Kenya shillings. Other currencies are shown only as approximations, using exchange rates that the administrator enters by hand, because no live exchange-rate service is used. The interface is written in English; other languages are provided by Google's free website-translation widget, which needs an internet connection, sends page text to Google when a language other than English is chosen, and may translate imperfectly (receipts, certificates and evidence documents are excluded from translation so they stay as issued). Notifications are sent by email and also shown inside the site, so nothing depends on email alone, but emails still need a configured mail server and there is no SMS.

The recommendation quality depends on the listings and attractions that are entered, and the Smart score weights are fixed in the code. The review anomaly detection is rule based and has not been tuned on real review data. Testing was done by the developer with sample data, including repeated automated end-to-end tests of the main flows and an access-control sweep of every page; no load testing and no user acceptance testing with real hosts and guests has been carried out.
