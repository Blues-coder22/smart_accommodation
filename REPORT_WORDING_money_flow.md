# Wording for the report: booking price and payment flow (Chapters 4 and 5)

**Booking price.** The host sets a price per night (and may add an on-site experience fee). When a tourist books, the system adds a *guest service fee* (a percentage set by the administrator) to the host's price, so the tourist's total is: stay + extras + service fee. Separately, the platform keeps a *commission* (also a percentage set by the administrator) of the host's price. The commission is not added to the tourist's bill; it is taken out of the host's price. Both percentages are saved on each booking, so changing them later only affects new bookings.

**Worked example.** 2 nights at KES 8,500 = KES 17,000. Service fee 5% = KES 850, so the tourist's total is KES 17,850. Commission 10% of KES 17,000 = KES 1,700. The tourist pays KES 15,300 to the host (KES 17,000 minus the commission) and KES 2,550 to the platform (KES 850 service fee + KES 1,700 commission).

**Payment flow.** The system does not process or hold money. The tourist pays two amounts, each directly to its owner (M-Pesa or bank transfer), and enters the reference of each payment on one payment page (an optional screenshot can be attached). (1) The host's share is paid to the host, who confirms it when it arrives (cash on arrival is allowed for this part only). (2) The platform's share is paid to the administrator, who compares it with the M-Pesa or bank statement and accepts or rejects it. Each confirmation sends the tourist an email and a printable receipt with a verification code. The host never owes the platform anything afterwards, and the platform never receives the host's money.

**Records and disputes.** Every payment change is logged (who, when, what, why). If a booking is cancelled after the platform's share was accepted, the administrator is warned and can record a refund. An administrator can print a dispute evidence pack for any booking.

**Limitations to state honestly.** Payments are recorded and verified by people, not automated: M-Pesa Daraja integration, automatic refunds and SMS verification are future work. Rates are changed by the administrator in Revenue & commission and apply to new bookings only. One account per role can be signed in at a time in one browser (use a private window for a second tourist).

**Privacy of identifiers.** Bookings are identified to users by a random public reference (for example BK-K7M2QX9TPA), never by their database number. Payment and receipt links carry encrypted, tamper-proof tokens that are tied to the type of record, so a guessed or altered link is refused, and every page still checks who is asking. Receipt numbers are derived with a secret key and do not reveal how many payments exist.

**Refunds.** If a booking is cancelled after the administrator has accepted the platform's share, the system records the refund due: in full when the host cancels or the dates are released automatically, in full when the guest cancels at least the free-cancellation period before check-in, and none automatically for a later cancellation. The guest, host and administrator are notified (in the site and by email); the administrator sends the refund and records it. The host's share is refunded by the host, following the cancellation rules.

**Notifications, currency and language.** Important events are shown in an in-site notification list as well as by email. Prices are stored in Kenya shillings; guests may view approximate prices in other currencies using rates set by the administrator, while payment and receipts remain in shillings. Other languages are offered through Google's free translation widget; legal documents are excluded from translation.

**Certificates and printed records.** The administrator can print a numbered certificate for a verified host (or guest) and for an approved listing; anyone can check the number on a public page, and it is valid only while the verification or approval stands. Guests, hosts and the administrator can print a booking and payment record showing the information each is entitled to, hosts can print a payment statement for a date range, and the administrator can print a revenue report and a dispute evidence pack.
